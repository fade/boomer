* Contributors

boomer is a fork of [[https://github.com/atgreen/pure-tls][pure-tls]] and is
distributed under the MIT license, unchanged from upstream. Contributions made
here are made under that same license, and each contributor retains copyright in
their own contributions. This file lists the contributing authors. It does not
alter the license, and the authoritative copyright notices are the ones carried
in the source files themselves.

** Anthony Green <green@moxielogic.com>

Original author of pure-tls, from which boomer is derived. The TLS 1.3 protocol
implementation, the post-quantum key exchange, the Encrypted Client Hello
support, the X.509 verification and the ACME client all originate in that work.
His copyright notice appears in the source files he authored.

** John C. Mallery <jcma@csail.mit.edu>

Contributed to pure-tls upstream, and that work is present here through the
fork, including: the record buffer pool and its allocation context, reduced
per-record allocation on the AEAD encrypt path, constant-time comparison in
PKCS#1 v1.5 signature verification, and fixes to TLS 1.3 PSK session resumption.
His copyright notice appears in =src/buffer-pool.lisp=. Commits are authored as
=John Mallery <jcmallery@gmail.com>=.

** Brian O'Reilly <fade@deepsky.com>

Copyright (c) 2026 Brian O'Reilly.

Contributed both upstream and in this fork. Upstream, among other work: an
opt-in hostname verification policy, rejection of embedded-NUL and non-LDH bytes
in hostname verification, failing closed on an unusable explicit CA source,
authenticated PSK resumption under verify-required, acceptance of the RFC 8737
id-pe-acmeIdentifier critical extension, keeping the account contact address out
of ACME registration logs, recovery from transient ACME errors, and X.509 and
handshake regression tests. In boomer: the opt-in strict-privacy hostname
policy, the resumable record layer together with its adoption path and partial
transport handling, a stream that refuses use once its connection has been
handed over, and related hardening.
